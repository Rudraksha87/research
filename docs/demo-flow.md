### Step 2: Explore the Dashboard

2. Click on the pre-loaded project: **"Sub-Quadratic Attention Mechanisms for Long-Context LLMs"**.

### Step 3: Inspect Multi-Dimensional Views
1. **Research Overview**: Review the AI hypothesis, multi-source search strategy, and executive summary.
2. **Papers Explorer**: View literature indexed from arXiv/PubMed with relevance scores and authors.
3. **Deep Analysis**: Inspect extracted methodologies, sample sizes, benchmark datasets, strengths, and limitations.
4. **Comparison Matrix**: View the structured table comparing FlashAttention-2 vs. Mamba vs. RingAttention across complexity, memory constraints, and hardware affinities.
5. **Research Gaps**: Examine critical open questions and bottlenecks highlighted with impact severities (High, Medium, Low).
6. **Future Directions**: Review AI-generated research proposals with feasibility scores.
7. **Synthesis Report**: Read the compiled academic review with table of contents and Markdown export.

## 🔬 2. Creating a New Autonomous Research Project

1. Click **+ New Research** in the sidebar.
2. Enter a research topic:
   - **Title**: `Direct Preference Optimization vs RLHF in Reasoning Models`
   - **Research Question / Query**: `How does DPO stability compare to PPO when fine-tuning math and logic chain-of-thought models?`
   - **Domain**: `Artificial Intelligence / Machine Learning`
3. Click **Start Research Pipeline**.
4. Watch the multi-step real-time progress indicators:
   - [x] Formulating Research Strategy
   - [x] Querying Academic Repositories
   - [x] Ranking & Deduplicating Papers
   - [x] Synthesizing Cross-Paper Comparisons
   - [x] Spotting Research Gaps & Future Hypotheses
   - [x] Assembling Final Literature Report
5. Navigate directly into the newly generated research project!
